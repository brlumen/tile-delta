# Map data and attribution

TileDelta filters and repackages vector tiles from the Protomaps Basemap. Each release identifies its source build date. The overview uses layers derived from OpenStreetMap and Natural Earth. [Protomaps builds](https://maps.protomaps.com/builds/), [upstream data notices](https://github.com/protomaps/basemaps/blob/main/LICENSE_DATA.md)

The OpenStreetMap-derived data in the published PMTiles archives and tile deltas is available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0. This includes coastline data derived from OpenStreetMap and prepared by osmdata.openstreetmap.de. [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), [coastline source](https://osmdata.openstreetmap.de/)

When displaying these maps, show **© OpenStreetMap contributors** and link the attribution to the OpenStreetMap copyright page. Keep the attribution and license notices when redistributing the map data. [Attribution guidelines](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines)

Natural Earth data is in the public domain. [Natural Earth terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)
